🎃 Haddonfield-only music

Put .mp3 files in this folder. They play ONLY when the WeatherStar location is
Haddonfield (type "Haddonfield" or "Haddonfield, IL" in WeatherTV's WeatherStar
location box) — on both WeatherStar 3000 and 4000.

No other city ever hears them: the normal playlist only reads music/ and
music/default/. If this folder has no .mp3, Haddonfield falls back to the
normal music.
