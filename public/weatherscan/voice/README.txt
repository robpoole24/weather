Weatherscan announcer clips (optional)

Drop an MP3 here named after the line it replaces, then add the name to clips.json.
Example: CurrentConditions.mp3  ->  clips.json: ["CurrentConditions"]

Names and what each one says (keep each clip 1-3 seconds, mono, 128 kbps is plenty):
  CurrentConditions            "Here are your current conditions."
  TextForecast                 "Here's your local forecast."
  DaypartForecast              "Here's your forecast for the next few hours."
  LocalDoppler                 "Here's your local Doppler radar."
  RegionalForecastConditions   "Here's a look at the regional forecast."
  LocalAirportConditions       "Here's a look at conditions at area airports."
  AllergyReport                "Here's your allergy report."

Any line without a clip is read by the browser's voice instead.
