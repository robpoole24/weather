🔔 Alert configuration

Alerts are sent via email and/or SMS. Set these Railway environment variables and redeploy — no registration needed.

MONITOR_EMAIL_TO Your email address
MONITOR_EMAIL_FROM e.g. alerts@send.altruisticapps.com (verified Resend domain, not Gmail)
RESEND_API_KEY From resend.com — 3,000 emails/month free
MONITOR_SMS_TO Your phone number e.g. +19206669979
TEXTBELT_API_KEY From textbelt.com — $10 for ~1,000 texts
Both channels are optional — set one or both. Alerts fire at most once per 30 minutes per issue.
