WeatherTV — Free Live Storm Chasers, Weather Forecasters & Live Radar

WeatherTV is a free, ad-free streaming hub for live severe weather coverage. Watch 130+ YouTube channels from storm chasers, professional meteorologists, and weather creators across the United States — organized into Weather Forecasters, Storm Chasers, and Other Weather Creators groups, with a Live Now view for channels currently broadcasting.

Also included: an interactive live NEXRAD radar with National Weather Service alerts, SPC severe weather outlooks, Mesoscale Discussions, and HRRR short-term forecast mode; a retro WeatherStar 4000 local forecast display; and a curated playlist of recent weather videos.

No account, no sign-up, no ads, and no personal data collected — ever.

WeatherTV is a curated weather content platform bringing together the best storm chasers, weather forecasters, and weather creators in one place.

This app is a passthrough — all content plays through official YouTube embeds, preserving view counts, ad revenue, and creator support exactly as if you watched directly on YouTube.

WeatherStar 4000+ is powered by weatherstar.netbymatt.com — a fantastic recreation of the classic 1990s Weather Channel experience.
