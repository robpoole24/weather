Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026  ·  Altruistic Apps
The short version: Weather TV does not collect, store, or sell any personal information about you. We don't track you, profile you, or share your data with anyone — ever. The only data that may ever be collected is anonymous, technical error information used solely to keep the app working properly.

1. Who We Are

Weather TV is a free app developed and operated by Altruistic Apps. The app aggregates live and on-demand weather content — storm chasers, forecasters, and weather creators — delivered through official YouTube embeds. We can be reached at contact@altruisticapps.com.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, request, or process any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Your browsing history or behavior across other apps or websites
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII) of any kind

Weather TV requires no account, no sign-up, and no login. We never will.

3. Information That May Be Collected

The only data that may ever be collected is anonymous technical diagnostics — for example, error logs that help us identify and fix crashes or bugs. This information:

  • Contains no personally identifiable information
  • Is never linked to any individual user
  • Is used solely for the purpose of improving app stability
  • Is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party

4. YouTube Content & Third-Party Services

Weather TV is a passthrough platform. All video content is played through official YouTube embeds, which means YouTube's own privacy policy and terms of service apply to the video player and any interactions with it. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy for details on how YouTube handles data.

We do not have access to any data YouTube may collect through its player. We receive no user data from YouTube as a result of your viewing activity.

5. Children's Privacy

Weather TV is designed to be appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13, or under the applicable age of digital consent in your region. Because we collect no personal data from any users at all, our practices are consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information through this app, please contact us at contact@altruisticapps.com and we will investigate promptly.

6. Content Advisory for Parents

Weather TV is a live content platform. The overwhelming majority of content consists of professional weather forecasting and storm chasing, and is entirely family-friendly. However, because some channels broadcast live in real-time field conditions, there is a small possibility that a creator may use language some viewers could find objectionable — for example, in a moment of genuine shock or surprise during a dangerous weather event.

This is not typical behavior on any of the featured channels, and based on extensive viewing, it is extremely rare. That said, parents of very young children should be aware that live broadcasts cannot be pre-screened or filtered in real time.

7. Data Selling & Advertising

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade any user data — because we don't have any to sell. Weather TV contains no behavioral advertising, no tracking pixels, and no third-party ad networks operated by us. Any ads that appear within YouTube's embedded player are served by YouTube under their own policies and are not placed by Altruistic Apps.

8. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to secure or that could be compromised in a breach. Our server infrastructure is used solely to serve the channel directory and manage live stream status updates.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated effective date. Our commitment to collecting no personal data will not change. Any material changes would also be communicated through the app.

10. Contact Us

Questions, concerns, or feedback about this privacy policy or Weather TV in general are always welcome.
