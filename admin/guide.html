Where a channel goes (first match wins): where you put it → its collection's block → its country / state code (IN, PK, BD → Asia · GB → Europe · AU → Australia · US-WI style state codes → Regional United States; a plain US stays put) → its group's block. Channels 1–10 are Weather TV's own (1 Live Now, 2 Forecast, 3 Live Radar, 4 WeatherStar, 5 Highlights, 6–10 playlists).
Drag or use ▲▼ to order a block; numbers count up from its start. Type a number to fix one (📌) — it sorts into place; two channels on one number show red until you change one. Move to puts a channel somewhere by hand; ↺ Automatic undoes that.

Groups & collections → blocks Send everyone in a group or collection to a block at once. This is also how the original layout's groups line up with the guide. A channel's own collections can instead be a Network (members get its badge and stay put — “… Chasers” by default) or Same creator (sister channels sit right after the lead — “… Channels” by default).
