Where does the money go?

All proceeds go toward the maintenance and running costs of our apps and websites: servers, data services and new features. It isn't a profit venture. It keeps the apps free for everyone.

Do you get my personal information?

No. Fourthwall handles checkout, payment and shipping. We never see your name, address or payment details, the same way our apps never collect your data.

Who makes the shirts?

Everything is printed on demand by Fourthwall and shipped straight to you, so nothing sits in a warehouse and nothing goes to waste.
